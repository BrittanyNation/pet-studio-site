Good to know

Drop-off is 7–9 AM for grooms. Pickup gets a text when your pet is ready — usually 2–3 hours for a full groom. Late pickup after 7 PM is $10, stated here rather than as a surprise fee.

Plain-word questions

What if my pet is nervous or reactive?

Say so in the notes — it changes the plan, not the welcome. Nervous pets get quieter slots, one handler, and breaks, and the team will say honestly if a different setting would suit them better.

Can I stay and watch the groom?

There is a viewing window for baths, and most pets settle faster when their owner steps out for the cut itself — the report card and photos cover the parts you miss.

What vaccinations are required?

Rabies for all pets, plus distemper and Bordetella for dogs. A clear photo of the record is accepted — the checklist above repeats this so it is never a door-time surprise.
