Services & Pricing • Willow Creek Pet Studio

Every price, before you book.

Prices vary by size and coat — so this page says exactly what moves a price up, by how much, before anyone books. No “call for a quote,” no add-ons appearing at pickup.

What moves a groom price up, stated plainly: heavy matting (+$15, quoted before work starts), extra-large breeds (+$12), and de-shedding treatment (+$10). If a coat needs more than quoted, the studio calls before doing — and charging — the extra work.

Notes for every kind of pet

PUPPIES
Under 6 months

Start small, start kind

Puppies start with the short intro groom and half-day daycare, then build up — the page says this before a new owner books a full day that would overwhelm a puppy.

SENIORS
Extra time, no rush

Senior pets move slower here

Senior grooms book with double time at no extra charge, with rest breaks and a non-slip mat — and the booking form asks about mobility and medication in plain words.

CATS
Cat-only hours

A quiet room of their own

Cat grooms and boarding happen in a dog-free room, first appointments of the day — the detail anxious cat owners look for is on this page, not buried in an FAQ.
