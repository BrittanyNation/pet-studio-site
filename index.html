Calm by design

One-pet-at-a-time grooming, small daycare groups sorted by size and energy, and quiet hours built into every boarding night — the calming details are content, not fine print.

Proof, not promises

A report card at every pickup and a photo every boarding night. The site promises only what the studio can show, and says exactly when each update arrives.

First visit, no guesswork

Vaccines, food, and comfort items are listed as a simple checklist on the booking page — so a first visit starts relaxed instead of with a scramble at the door.

Design notes

Three worries, three pages

Home answers “can I trust them,” Services answers “what does it cost, all-in,” and Book a Visit answers “what happens next” — one job per page, with hours and services repeated in the hero so no page depends on another.

Pricing as reassurance

Starting prices live on the cards, full tables live on the services page, and add-ons are priced before booking — an anxious owner should never have to ask what the total might be.

Warm, still calm

Deep teal carries the structure, mint softens the trust accents, and butter marks the moments that matter — badges and selected states always pair color with words, never color alone.
