# Willow Creek Pet Studio — Multi-Page Business Site UI Concept

An original multi-page business website UI concept for a fictional pet grooming, daycare & boarding studio: a home page built around trust and clear starting prices, a services & pricing page where every price and add-on is stated before booking, and a book-a-visit page with a calm first-visit form, a what-to-bring checklist, and a travelling order summary.

**Live demo:** https://brittanynation.github.io/pet-studio-site/

- Services & Pricing: https://brittanynation.github.io/pet-studio-site/services.html

## Roles this supports

Built to support applications for **UI Designer**, **Front-End Designer**, **Web Designer**, and **Junior UI/UX Designer** roles — small-business website work that sits between visual design and front-end implementation.

## What it demonstrates

- **Business-site information architecture** — one job per page: Home ("can I trust them"), Services & Pricing ("what does it cost, all-in"), Book a Visit ("what happens next")
- **Pricing as reassurance** — starting prices on the service cards, full tables on the pricing page, what moves a price up stated with amounts, and add-ons priced before booking rather than at pickup
- **Trust through specifics** — a labeled at-a-glance band (staff ratio, photo promise, no-surprise rule), a report card at every pickup, and first-visit requirements listed as a checklist instead of fine print
- **Designed states, not just screens** — pre-filled sample pet details, a checked de-shedding add-on reflected in the summary, and a total that repeats on the submit button
- **Honest form design** — a mock booking form that says what contact details will be used for before submission, marks notes as optional, and states plainly that no real booking happens
- **Accessibility-minded choices** — contrast-safe teal/mint/butter pairings, badges and states that use words, never color alone, skip link, `aria-current` navigation, real `<table>` elements for pricing, native `<details>` FAQs, and real labels on every field
- **Responsive layout** — stat, service, and note grids step down cleanly on small screens, pricing tables scroll horizontally while remaining readable on phones, and the visit summary stacks below the form on mobile
- **Semantic, framework-free build** — hand-written HTML/CSS, one stylesheet, no JavaScript required, no external images; all visuals are CSS-only
